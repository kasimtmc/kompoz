| `rememberSaveable`, `rememberSaveableStateHolder` / `SaveableStateProvider` | ✅ | inside a `SaveableStateHolder` the value is kept while its content is not shown (nav entries, saved tabs, items scrolled out of LazyColumn/LazyRow, by their key); `rememberScrollState`, `rememberLazyListState`, `rememberPagerState`, `rememberNavController`, `rememberTextFieldState` are saveable.
