The licenses of what an app built with Kompoz contains. They are copied next to the app with its
other assets (into the APK on Android, into the page's data on the web): keep them when you ship it.

  Kompoz.txt, NOTICE.txt          Kompoz: Apache License 2.0, and its NOTICE (which the license asks
                                  to go along with it)
  OFL.txt                         the Noto Sans fonts (assets/fonts)
  FreeType-FTL.txt, HarfBuzz.txt, the vendored libraries, in builds that contain them (Android, iOS,
  libpng.txt, zlib.txt, SDL.txt   Windows, the web; SDL not on Android)
  VulkanMemoryAllocator.txt       the GPU renderer's Vulkan backend's allocator (Linux, Android,
                                  Windows)

Kompoz.txt and NOTICE.txt are copies of LICENSE and NOTICE at the repository's root.
